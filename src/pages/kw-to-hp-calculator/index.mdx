Motors are rated in kilowatts in most of the world and in horsepower in North America. Both measure the mechanical power at the shaft, so the conversion is a fixed ratio. What is not fixed is which horsepower you mean, and how much electrical power the motor draws to deliver it.

## Formulas

<div class="formula">hp = kW × 1000 / 745.7 (mechanical)<br/>kW = hp × 745.7 / 1000<br/>Input kW = shaft kW / efficiency</div>

One mechanical (imperial) horsepower is 745.7 W, so 1 kW is 1.341 hp. Metric horsepower, written PS or CV, is 735.5 W, so 1 kW is 1.360 PS. The difference is about 1.4 %, which matters when you read a European nameplate.

## Worked example

A 7.5 kW motor is 7,500 / 745.7 = 10.06 hp, usually called a 10 hp motor.

A 10 hp motor delivers 10 × 745.7 = 7,457 W at the shaft. If its efficiency is 90 %, the electrical input is 7.457 / 0.9 = 8.29 kW.

## From horsepower to amps

Horsepower rates the shaft, but the supply carries the electrical input, and a motor also has losses and a power factor. For sizing under the NEC, do not calculate the current from horsepower. Use the full-load current in Table 430.250, which the <a href="/motor-circuit-calculator/">motor circuit calculator</a> reads for you. A 10 hp three-phase motor on 460 V has a table full-load current of 14 A. For a measured or nameplate load, use the <a href="/kw-to-amps-calculator/">kW to amps calculator</a> with the input power and the power factor.

## Common mistakes

- **Using shaft power as the electrical input.** The motor draws more than it delivers. A 90 % efficient motor draws 11 % more than its shaft output.
- **Mixing mechanical and metric horsepower.** A "10 hp" label in Europe is often 10 PS, which is 7.35 kW, not 7.46 kW.
- **Using horsepower for the circuit.** The NEC sizes motor conductors and protection from table current, not from a calculated value.
- **Forgetting that kW on an IEC nameplate is the shaft output.** The input is higher by the efficiency.

## Questions

### How many kW is 1 hp?
0.7457 kW for mechanical horsepower, or 0.7355 kW for metric horsepower.

### How many hp is 1 kW?
1.341 mechanical hp, or 1.360 metric horsepower.

### How many amps is a 10 hp motor?
It depends on voltage and phase. On a three-phase 460 V supply the NEC table value is 14 A, and on 230 V it is 28 A.

### Is a 7.5 kW motor a 10 hp motor?
Yes, within about 0.6 %. 7.5 kW is 10.06 hp.
